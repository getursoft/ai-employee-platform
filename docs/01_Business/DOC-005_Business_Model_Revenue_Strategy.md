# 1. Executive Summary

The AI Employee Platform will operate as a B2B SaaS platform where businesses subscribe to AI Employees rather than purchasing traditional software licenses.

Revenue will come from multiple recurring streams including platform subscriptions, AI Employee licensing, usage-based billing, enterprise services, and an AI Employee Marketplace.

The objective is to maximize Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) while maintaining a scalable, cloud-native business model.

---

# 2. Business Philosophy

Traditional SaaS sells software.

AI Employee Platform sells digital employees.

Customers are not buying features.

Customers are buying completed work.

---

# 3. Revenue Streams

The platform will generate revenue from six primary sources.

Customers subscribe to access the AI Employee Platform.

- User Management
- AI Employee Management
- Security
- Analytics
- Workflow Engine

Recurring monthly subscription.

# 13. Long-Term Revenue Vision

The long-term objective is to evolve from selling individual AI Employees to operating the world's largest AI Employee Marketplace.

Businesses will hire, manage, and monitor AI Employees through a single enterprise platform.

The marketplace will become a recurring revenue engine similar to enterprise app stores.

# 16. Conclusion

The AI Employee Platform is designed around recurring revenue rather than one-time software sales.

Every new AI Employee, customer, integration, and marketplace partner increases the long-term value of the platform.

This creates a scalable business model capable of supporting long-term global growth.
