# 1 Executive Summary

The AI Employee Platform is an enterprise platform for creating intelligent AI Employees capable of performing complete business operations through natural conversations.

The platform provides reusable infrastructure while individual AI Employees implement industry-specific business logic.

The first AI Employee developed on the platform is Travel AI.

---

# 2 Product Vision

Replace traditional business software with intelligent AI Employees.

Instead of interacting with software screens, users interact with AI Employees through:

- Voice
- WhatsApp
- Email
- Web Chat
- Mobile
- Teams
- Slack

The AI Employee completes work autonomously.

---

# 3 Product Goals

Primary Goals

- Reduce manual work
- Improve customer experience
- Increase automation
- Reduce operational cost
- Improve business productivity

Secondary Goals

- Platform reusability
- Multi-industry support
- Enterprise scalability
